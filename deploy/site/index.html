Kolf

Miniature golf with friends: sloped greens, sand traps, bridges, windmills, and black holes. Play on one computer or online with a join code.

Play online

Open Kolf and choose Play Online. It connects to kolf.pro automatically. Host a game and share its code, or enter a friend's code to join.
