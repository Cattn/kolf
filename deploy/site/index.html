Kolf

A miniature golf game for Windows and Linux. Play a course by yourself, take turns with people in the room, or play with friends online.

Download

Kolf is free and open source.

Download Kolf

Playing online

Choose Play Online in Kolf and it connects to kolf.pro on its own. Host a game and send your friends the code, or enter the code a friend sent you.
