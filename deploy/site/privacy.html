Privacy

Kolf has no accounts and sends no telemetry.

What the kolf.pro server handles

  • The display names and colors you choose, the course, and match state, only while your lobby exists. Nothing is stored after the lobby closes.
  • Your IP address, in memory, to limit connections and wrong join codes per address.

Logs

The server logs events such as "lobby created" or "match finished" with random IDs, durations, and player counts. Logs do not contain display names, join codes, or IP addresses, and are kept for about 7 days.

The web server in front of kolf.pro may keep standard access logs for up to 7 days to keep the service running and secure.
